The Gallery and Ready wireframes are explicitly labeled reconstructions based on supplied finished screens. They are not represented as original Figma exports.

The supplied files do not include individual finished Searching, Connecting, My Recordings detail, expanded Sound, or expanded User Warnings screens. The case study uses the available states and identifies those gaps in its copy. The supplied red detection screen visibly says “Level Risk 1” despite using a red risk state; its external case-study label is “High risk” until the source artwork is corrected.

The Survue homepage image remains a placeholder. The project record has `contentStatus: "draft"` and identifies incomplete fields in `placeholderFields`.
