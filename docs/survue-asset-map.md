## Original versus reconstructed artifacts

Welcome, Pair, Home, Detection, and Settings are original wireframes. Ready and Gallery are retrospective low-fidelity reconstructions from final screens. They appear in their own explicitly labelled story, never mixed into the original process board. No new artifacts or research are fabricated.

## Evidence boundaries

The existing record states founding product design and C# implementation, with Figma, C#, and .NET. It does not document team composition, exact implementation ownership, a production release, road testing, detection thresholds or accuracy, or measured safety outcomes. The copy preserves the established contribution and explicitly separates interface evidence from these unverified claims.

The red detection screen reads “Level Risk 1” despite its red warning treatment. The case study labels the visuals by color and discloses that severity naming requires correction before release. Source screenshots are not altered. Warning comprehension, non-color cues, and connection-loss behavior are presented as future validation priorities, not completed work.

Individual Searching, Connecting, My Recordings detail, expanded Sound, and expanded User Warnings screens are not available. Their absence is documented here rather than narrated as asset-production notes in the portfolio. The project remains a draft.

## Confirmed visual foundations

The user confirmed the founder defined the palette and Helvetica Neue. Palette: dark background #000000; dark options card #212020; light background #F2F2F2; light card #FFFFFF; safe passage #0FA958; level-one risk #EAC234; red road/vehicle warning and founder-directed onboarding action #E4002B. The designer selected icons through Iconify's Figma plugin. Original PNG exports are preserved under `public/images/projects/survue/foundations/`.

The components show only exported states: filled primary action, outlined device alternative, collapsed/expanded Theme settings row, and one toggle state. No disabled, pressed, or loading variants are fabricated. Helvetica Neue examples demonstrate the confirmed family, not original type sizes or weights. The user requested a standard mobile spacing hierarchy: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32 px. It is clearly labelled a proposed guideline rather than original Figma measurements.
